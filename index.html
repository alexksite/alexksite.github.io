Every type of chatbot

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions business owners ask before starting a chatbot, answered directly.

What does Alex Koziy build?

Custom chatbots in Python for business. They run on WhatsApp, Instagram Direct, Telegram and websites, and handle customer support, FAQs, lead capture, orders and bookings. They can use AI (Claude, OpenAI) for natural conversation, with a rule-based fallback and instant handoff to a human when needed.

Which channels do the chatbots work on?

WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API, Twilio or 360dialog), Instagram Direct, Telegram, and your own website. One codebase can serve several channels, so you are not locked into a single platform.

Why custom Python instead of a no-code builder like ManyChat?

No-code builders charge a monthly subscription that grows with your audience, limit you to preset blocks, and keep your data on their servers. A custom Python bot runs on your own server for a few dollars a month regardless of traffic, supports any logic you can describe, integrates directly with your CRM, database and payment tools, and — most importantly — you own the source code and the data.

Can the chatbot use AI like ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes. The bots integrate directly with AI models such as Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI for natural, context-aware conversation. AI answers are backed by a rule-based fallback, so if the AI is unavailable the bot keeps working instead of going silent.

Do I own the chatbot and its data?

Yes. You receive the full source code and the bot runs on your own cloud account, so the customer data stays with you. There is no platform lock-in and nothing to lose if a third-party builder changes its pricing or policy.

How much does it cost to run?

Running costs stay low. The bot is deployed to the cloud platform you prefer — your own AWS, Google Cloud, a small VPS, or another host — and because it's a lightweight service it runs on modest resources, typically only a few dollars a month regardless of how many customers message you. You pay once for development, you own the code, and any AI usage you enable is billed to you at cost, with no platform markup.

Can the chatbot connect to my CRM, database or payment system?

Yes. Because it is real Python code, the bot talks directly to REST APIs, databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB) and business systems — no fragile Zapier or Make middle-layer and no extra subscriptions.

What happens after the bot is launched?

The bots ship with health checks, structured logging and monitoring, and I offer ongoing support and maintenance. Every unanswered question is logged so the bot's knowledge keeps improving over time. It is a maintained product, not a hand-off-and-vanish delivery.

Can you build a chatbot for trading or finance?

Yes. I develop applications in the finance area and follow trading and cryptocurrency closely. A live example is my WhatsApp Trading FAQ bot, which teaches trading concepts, indicators and risk management. Note: these bots educate — they do not give buy or sell advice.

Do you also do other kinds of software?

My focus is Python chatbots. If you're curious about the background behind that, I have 20+ years in enterprise software — see my enterprise background and ZK & Keikai work.
