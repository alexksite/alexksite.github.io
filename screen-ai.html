On-Screen AI Tutor for coding and quizzes

A small desktop app I built that puts a floating helper on top of any window. One click and it reads what's on your screen with Claude Haiku vision — then explains the answer with a confidence score, or outlines a coding approach. No copy-pasting into a chat window, no switching tabs. It's a study, brainstorming and productivity companion — and a demo of my Vision AI and desktop-app work.

How it works

One click. It understands your screen.

Two buttons float above everything. Each one captures the screen and sends the image to Claude Haiku, which reads the context and answers — fast and cheap, because Haiku is optimised for exactly this.

Explain & check yourself

On a quiz or study question, one click captures the screen and returns the most likely answer with a confidence percentage and a short reason — so you can verify your own thinking, not just copy it.

Understand a coding task

On a coding problem, the second button reads the task straight from the image and returns a worked approach and sample solution with a complexity note — a way to learn the pattern without alt-tabbing to a chat.

This is a learning and productivity tool — for checking your own work, unblocking yourself and exploring alternative approaches. Please use it responsibly and within the rules of any exam, course or assessment you take part in.

How it's built

The stack behind it

A lightweight desktop overlay plus a multimodal model — kept small, fast and cheap to run.

  • Floating desktop overlay

    An always-on-top window with two buttons, built in Python, that sits over any application without getting in the way.

  • One-click screen capture

    Captures the current screen on demand (mss / Pillow) and sends the image — no manual copy-paste, no re-typing questions.

  • Claude Haiku vision

    Uses Anthropic's Claude Haiku for multimodal understanding — chosen because it's very fast and low-cost, ideal for quick on-screen help.

  • Transparent output

    Returns the answer with a confidence percentage and a short explanation, so the result is auditable rather than a black box.
