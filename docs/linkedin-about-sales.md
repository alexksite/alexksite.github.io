- **First line = lost revenue happening now** — the strongest hook; it's what
  shows before "…see more".
- **Speaks about the client, not the tech** — every point is a benefit ("→").
- **Covers all bot types** (matches the site) without sounding "AI-only".
- **Kills the main objection** (growing no-code fees) with your real advantage:
  pay once, own the code, $0 hosting offer.
- **Proof, not claims** — invites them to message a live bot and see pricing.
- **Low-friction CTA** ("free, no pressure") — raises reply rate.
