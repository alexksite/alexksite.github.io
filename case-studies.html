Orion Restaurant Instagram Bot

An AI-powered Instagram Direct assistant for a restaurant: menu consultation, FAQs and order help, with a rule-based fallback and a manager handoff mode. Because the AI answers in whatever language it's asked, it isn't limited to one — it replies to each customer in their own language.

Channel
Instagram Direct (Meta API)
Value
Answers menu questions and helps take orders, freeing up staff
Languages
Any — the AI replies in the customer's own language
More details
  • AI conversations via Claude with a rule-based fallback — zero downtime
  • Menu and knowledge base in a simple config — update prices without code changes
  • Manager handoff: the bot goes silent while a human takes over, then resumes
  • Conversation history kept for natural, context-aware replies
  • AI replies in the customer's own language — not limited to English or Ukrainian
